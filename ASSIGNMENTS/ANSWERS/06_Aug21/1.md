# Assignment 06 — Employee Management System (Queries)

## Table Creation

```sql
CREATE TABLE employees (
    employee_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    employee_name VARCHAR(50),
    department VARCHAR(30),
    city VARCHAR(30),
    salary INT,
    joining_date DATE,
    experience INT,
    age INT,
    gender ENUM('MALE', 'FEMALE', 'OTHER'),
    employment_type ENUM('FULL_TIME', 'PART_TIME', 'CONTRACT', 'INTERN'),
    skills SET('JAVA', 'PYTHON', 'SQL', 'REACT', 'SPRING'),
    email VARCHAR(100),
    manager_id INT
);
```

## 1. Employees from IT, Finance, or HR who joined after '2023-01-01', salary ₹40,000–₹90,000, experience > 3

```sql
SELECT * FROM employees
WHERE department IN ('IT', 'Finance', 'HR')
  AND joining_date > '2023-01-01'
  AND salary BETWEEN 40000 AND 90000
  AND experience > 3;
```

## 2. Employees whose email is NULL, salary > ₹50,000, and joining date after '2022-01-01'

```sql
SELECT * FROM employees
WHERE email IS NULL
  AND salary > 50000
  AND joining_date > '2022-01-01';
```

## 3. Employees whose manager_id is NOT NULL, department not HR, salary below ₹40,000 or above ₹80,000

```sql
SELECT * FROM employees
WHERE manager_id IS NOT NULL
  AND department != 'HR'
  AND (salary < 40000 OR salary > 80000);
```

## 5. FULL_TIME employees with JAVA or PYTHON, salary > ₹50,000, email NOT NULL


## 30. Complex filtering ordered by department ASC, salary DESC

```sql

WHERE (employee_name LIKE 'S%' OR employee_name LIKE '%a%')
  AND department IN ('IT', 'Finance')
  AND joining_date BETWEEN '2023-01-01' AND '2026-12-31'
  AND salary BETWEEN 40000 AND 100000
  AND experience > 2
  AND email IS NOT NULL

ORDER BY department ASC, salary DESC;
```
