# Assignment 01 — Employee Management System

## QNO 1: Employee Management System

### Task

Create an `employees` table and insert at least 15 employees with different values.

### Requirements

- At least 4 departments: IT, HR, Finance, Sales
- At least 5 cities: Indore, Bhopal, Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore
- Salary range: ₹30,000 to ₹1,00,000
- Experience range: 1 to 10 years
- Age range: 22 to 40 years
- Joining dates: 2020 to 2026
- Some employees with the same department
- Some employees with the same city
- Some employees with similar salaries
- Employees joined in different years

---

## 1. Check Current Database

```sql
SELECT DATABASE();
```

## 2. Create Employees Table

```sql
CREATE TABLE employees (
    emp_id INT,
    emp_name VARCHAR(20),
    department VARCHAR(20),
    city VARCHAR(10),
    salary INT,
    experience INT,
    age INT,
    joining_date DATE,
    sample_date DATE
);
```
