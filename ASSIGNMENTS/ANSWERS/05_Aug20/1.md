# Assignment 01 — Product Management System

## Table: products

## 1. Create Products Table

```sql
CREATE TABLE products (
    product_id INT,
    product_name VARCHAR(100),
    category VARCHAR(50),
    brand VARCHAR(50),
    city VARCHAR(50),
    price DECIMAL(10,2),
    stock INT,
    rating DECIMAL(2,1),
    manufacturing_date DATE
);
```

# SQL Queries

## 5. Price Greater Than ₹30,000, Rating at Least 4, Exclude Mumbai

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE price > 30000
  AND rating >= 4
  AND city != "Mumbai";
```

## 6. Stock Less Than 20 OR Price Greater Than ₹75,000, Category Not Grocery

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE (stock < 20 OR price > 75000)
  AND category != "Grocery";
```

## 7. Price Between ₹15,000 and ₹60,000, Rating Between 3 and 5, Manufactured After 2022

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE price BETWEEN 15000 AND 60000
  AND rating BETWEEN 3 AND 5
  AND manufacturing_date > "2022-12-31";
```

## 8. From Indore, Pune, or Delhi, Price Greater Than ₹20,000, Stock Not 0

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE city IN ("Indore", "Pune", "Delhi")
  AND price > 20000
  AND stock != 0;
```

## 9. Products Belonging to Electronics or Furniture

## 10. Products Belonging to Electronics, Clothing, or Grocery

## 14. Stock Between 20 and 60

## 15. Rating Between 3 and 5 and Price Greater Than ₹20,000

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE rating BETWEEN 3 AND 5
```

## 16. Manufactured Between 2023 and 2025, Electronics or Furniture

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date BETWEEN "2023-01-01" AND "2025-12-31"
  AND category IN ("Electronics", "Furniture");
```

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
```

## 21. Electronics or Furniture, Stock Greater Than 10, Price Less Than ₹60,000, Manufactured After 2022

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE category IN ("Electronics", "Furniture")
  AND stock > 10
  AND price < 60000
  AND manufacturing_date > "2022-12-31";
```

## 22. Name Contains "a", Price NOT ₹10,000–₹50,000, Stock Greater Than 5

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE product_name LIKE "%a%"
  AND price NOT BETWEEN 10000 AND 50000
  AND stock > 5;
```

## 23. Manufactured 2022–2025, Electronics/Clothing/Furniture, Rating Greater Than 3, Not Mumbai

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date BETWEEN "2022-01-01" AND "2025-12-31"
  AND category IN ("Electronics", "Clothing", "Furniture")
  AND rating > 3
  AND city != "Mumbai";
```

## 24. Name Starts With S or M, Not Indore or Mumbai, Price ₹15,000–₹80,000, Rating at Least 4, Manufactured After 2023

```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE (product_name LIKE "S%" OR product_name LIKE "M%")
  AND city NOT IN ("Indore", "Mumbai")
  AND price BETWEEN 15000 AND 80000
  AND rating >= 4
  AND manufacturing_date > "2023-12-31";
```
