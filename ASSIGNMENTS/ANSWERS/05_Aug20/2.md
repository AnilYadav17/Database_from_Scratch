# Assignment 02 — Product Management System (Date Queries)

## 1. Products manufactured after '2024-01-01'
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date > '2024-01-01';
```

## 2. Products manufactured before '2023-01-01'
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date < '2023-01-01';
```

## 3. Products manufactured between '2022-01-01' and '2024-12-31'
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date BETWEEN '2022-01-01' AND '2024-12-31';
```

## 4. Products manufactured after '2023-01-01' whose price is greater than ₹20,000
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date > '2023-01-01'
  AND price > 20000;
```

## 5. Products manufactured between '2023-01-01' and '2025-12-31' whose rating is at least 4
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date BETWEEN '2023-01-01' AND '2025-12-31'
  AND rating >= 4;
```

## 6. Products manufactured after '2023-01-01' whose stock is greater than 10
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date > '2023-01-01'
  AND stock > 10;
```

## 7. Products manufactured before '2024-01-01' OR whose price is greater than ₹80,000
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date < '2024-01-01'
   OR price > 80000;
```

## 8. Products manufactured between '2023-01-01' and '2025-12-31' whose stock is not 0
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date BETWEEN '2023-01-01' AND '2025-12-31'
  AND stock != 0;
```

## 9. Products manufactured after '2022-01-01' whose price is between ₹15,000 and ₹60,000 and rating is at least 3
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date > '2022-01-01'
  AND price BETWEEN 15000 AND 60000
  AND rating >= 3;
```

## 10. Products manufactured before '2024-01-01' whose rating is less than 3 OR whose stock is 0
```sql
SELECT * FROM products
WHERE manufacturing_date < '2024-01-01'
  AND (rating < 3 OR stock = 0);
```

## 11. Products belonging to Electronics or Furniture that were manufactured after '2023-01-01'

## 12. Products belonging to Electronics, Clothing, or Grocery that were manufactured between '2022-01-01' and '2025-12-31'

## 30. Products whose name starts with S OR contains phone, category is Electronics or Furniture, manufacturing date is between '2023-01-01' and '2026-12-31', price is between ₹20,000 and ₹90,000, rating is at least 4, stock is greater than 0, city is Indore, Pune, or Delhi, and brand is NOT Samsung, LG, or Sony
```sql

  AND price BETWEEN 20000 AND 90000
  AND rating >= 4
  AND stock > 0
  AND city IN ('Indore', 'Pune', 'Delhi')
  AND brand NOT IN ('Samsung', 'LG', 'Sony');
```
